Question about Starbuck gift cards?

Dive into FAQ or review ourCard Terms and Conditionfor more information

Do Starbucks gift cards expire?

Starbucks gift cards do not have an expiration date, nor does the value on your gift card ever expire.

What is the security code used for? Where is the security code on a Starbucks gift card?

The security code ensures that only the person in possession of the physical card is able to use the gift card. You can find the security code on the back of your Starbucks Card.

Can I check my balance without the security code?

You may check your gift card balance without the security code only if it is registered with your Starbucks Rewards account. Once registered,
you can view your balance in the following ways:

Online: Visit our Card page and select "Manage" to view the card balances.
Starbucks app: Select the "Scan" tab to view balances on all of the cards associated with your Starbucks Rewards account.

For new or unregistered cards, you must provide the security code. Visit our Card page to get started.

Why is my Starbucks gift card balance not showing?

Your gift card balance will automatically refresh every 4 hours.

How can I reload my Starbucks gift card?

You can reload your Starbucks gift card at participating Starbucks locations, by visiting our card page online or on the Starbucks app. You can also
set up Auto Reload, which allows you to set an amount on your registered Starbucks Card to reload when the balance falls below a set
amount that you choose. You can set up Auto Reload or change your Auto Reload settings by logging into your Starbucks account and selecting "Manage". These
options are also available within the Starbucks app. If you're having trouble reloading your gift card: Check your billing information: Ensure that the name and address entered on your account matches exactly what your financial institution
has on file for you. If you recently moved or changed your name, make sure to also change it in your billing information. Check your credit card information: Make sure your credit card is current with a valid expiration date, and you are entering the correct
security code. Other ways to reload your card include PayPal and Apple Pay. If you are still experiencing trouble, contact your financial institution for additional assistance. Starbucks® Rewards members can enjoy a Digital Starbucks Card Reload Bonus Star Benefit: Digital Starbucks Card Reload Bonus Star Benefit: Digitally reload $30 to your Starbucks Card in your Starbucks account to receive 10 Bonus
Stars or $50 to receive 25 Bonus Stars. Max 25 Bonus Stars per transaction. At participating stores. .

Where can I buy physical Starbucks gift cards?

You can find a selection of physical Starbucks gift cards by visiting Starbucks retail stores, various grocery, drug, and national retailers, as well as
online retailers such as Amazon.com.

If you would like to purchase an eGift (a digital Starbucks gift card), visit www.starbucks.com/gift. Digital gift cards may also be purchased in
the app by selecting the Gift icon at the bottom of the splash page upon opening the app. You can also send an eGift through iMessage, which you can learn more about here.

Can I buy a Starbucks gift card online?

Physical Starbucks gift cards will soon be available via Starbucks Shop! Until then, you may purchase an eGift (a digital Starbucks gift card) on www.starbucks.com/gift or Amazon. Digital gift cards may also be purchased in the app by selecting the Gift icon at the bottom of the splash page upon opening the app. Physical cards are also available at our coffeehouses and other retailers.

Can I send a Starbucks gift card by text or via other messaging and social media apps?

Yes! You can send Starbucks gift cards by text through the Starbucks app. Launch the app, select the Gift tab, and choose your design. Once
you input the recipient name and your gift amount, you can choose to send your eGift via text. After checkout, you will be able to send a link
to your recipient through any messaging app. This includes text message, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Slack, etc.

If you have an iPhone, you can send a Starbucks gift card directly through the Messages app! Once you have the Starbucks app installed on
your device, you can start a new message or open an existing one, click the + button next to the message field and select Starbucks Gifts to
choose a design and dollar amount. Send a gift card to anyone, whether they’re an iPhone or Android user! They’ll be able to enjoy their gift.
Note: gift cards cannot be sent in group messages.

About eGift cards

A Starbucks eGift card (also known as an “eGift”) is a Starbucks Gift Card that is purchased and sent digitally.

For Senders of an eGift, go to eGift History when signed in to view, send, or resend eGifts you’ve purchased — or, to directly contact Starbucks eGift Support for help with your order.

About Starbucks Gift Cards in general

Starbucks Gift Cards, including eGifts, can be used to pay in a Starbucks café or to join the Starbucks® Rewards loyalty program.

Register Gift Cards and eGifts to a Starbucks® Rewards account and Starbucks® Rewards members earn Stars on qualifying purchases when you pay with that card.
